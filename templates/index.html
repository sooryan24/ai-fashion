FashionAI FAQs

What can FashionAI create?

FashionAI turns your outfit ideas into detailed fashion concepts with garment descriptions, styling tips, color guidance, and occasion suggestions.

How should I describe my design?

Include the style, colors, occasion, season, fit, and mood you want. The more specific your prompt, the more tailored the result.

Do I need an API key?

A configured API key is required for live AI generations. The app can still run in demo mode with sample designs.

Are the product matches available for purchase?

Product matches are suggestions from popular online stores. Check each retailer for current prices, availability, and shipping details.

Can I save designs for later?

Yes. Use the save action on a generated design to keep it in your local gallery in this browser.
